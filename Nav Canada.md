Original application date: Nov. 2021.
This was during the covid hiring freeze and I later received an email to complete the initial pre-assessment test on May 22nd, 2022. I completed the pre-assessment test the same day was achieved the minimum passing score. 

On Oct. 25th, 2022 I was invited to complete FEAST. I was originally applying for the Winnipeg FIR. The test was set for Nov. 2022.

Later into Nov. of 2022 I received the dreaded email that I did not qualify for either the FSS or ATC positions. I would need to wait to reapply for 3 years. I received this email on the day of my birthday in 2022!

Although disappointed, I was happy at the time that I attempted to take the test and achieve the dream of being an ATC.

During the time of the test and the next few years after I worked in Banking. Working at a call centre I started as a CCR, moving quickly into FSR position and finally into an FSA position in Dec. of 2024.

In this position, I excelled greatly. I was given the change to train, shadow, and support my peers on the team through a real time support program called, Peer Support. This position was given to me and 2 others (much more experienced with the Bank) due to my ability to quickly learn, and follow SOP's. My management team had a lot of faith in the actions I would take on a daily basis.

This is probably where the ATC dream would end, it was not on my radar in anyway, shape, or form since that original decline in 2022. However, like fate. One day on LinkedIn I came across a article for NavCanada and this got me wondering... I have changed so much over the past few years. My ability to multicast and take on pressure has truly been tested and I have done well.

I was waiting for the Toronto FIR to open up, and on June 11th, 2026 I reapplied to an ATC position for Toronto. I wanted Toronto as I had just bought a house ~1 hour outside of the GTA and my wife has a stable job with our 3 year old son. Another on the way. I know that a VFR position might result in a decline as we may be unable to relocate, however, the dream of IFR was still there.

----

On June 15th, 2026 I completed the pre-assessment once again and got the invitation to complete FEAST once again. I booked July 13th, 2026 - Taking a flex day at the bank.

While preparing I came across a few resources. One is now linked directly through NavCanada when they send out the FEAST email - https://feast-training.eurocontrol.int/en/ and people online also saying the "SkyTest" was another resource. I did not personally find the need to buy skytest. With the amount of resources online in general all of the information is available. You just need a plan.

I still have some knowledge on the different tests that were present so hopefully that will enable me to do better this time around.

The format is a touch different. In 2022 they allowed us to write both 1 & 2 in the same sitting before being told if we made it or now, however, with the next method you write the first test. Only if you meet the minimum will you move to the second test. After the second test, only if you pass you will move to the pre-screening interview.

----

July 13th, 2026 I passed the two FEAST tests and completed my pre-screening interview.

Many of the people on the test day were friendly and I wished I captured a few of their numbers throughout the process. Thankfully, I was the first to complete both tests, which had me nearly shitting my pants as I thought I failed both times.

When I finished pt. 2 I was given a red card. My first thought was, "Oh fuck. At least I can try again in 2 years.". Being the first one out, I felt a lot of stress while I waited for the results. I received the "congratulations" by one of the test administrators. Since I was the first done, I managed to get the pre-screening done quickly.

With the pre-screening I was told that I would receive follow up within the week if I have been successful in moving forward. These were very simply questions overall and I still fail to see the value in the questions to this day.

I managed to wait around with a few of the other test takers to congratulate them as I found the connection was quite strong at that point. Nearly everyone we had in a group after FEAST 1 managed to pass FEAST 2. I think this is where the connections start to build as you learn about people and their experiences while waiting for FEAST 2 to begin.

Overall, we started the day with ~80 people. ~30 people made it through FEAST 1 and when I left to drive home I seen ~15 people that passed FEAST 2. The number might have been much higher but I didn't take a full inventory. From everything I seen online, this was likely a very high pass rate group which is not the norm when it comes to the FEAST tests.

---

Forward to July 21st, 2026. I was at the cottage with my family when I received the email saying that I was successful in the testing. This email outlined that although success on the test, you are not guaranteed the progress to the next step.

My status on workday moved to "Background Check" which I took as a good sign. On July 27th, 2026 I received an email from Nav Canada stating that I must complete the MINTZ background check before July 29th, 2026. I have yet to receive the email though. I ended up sending an email to the careers inbox for additional support. Hopefully, I will continue to have some good news today.

And like magic, when I finished typing this out I received a response back from the careers email and the link needed to complete the background check! Onwards and upwards to hopefully receiving an invite to the next in-person assessment! Back ground check fully submitted on July 28th, 2026.

---

August 11th, 2026 the status in workday updated to "Interview". I imagine this is a really good sign and hopefully the interview link and info can come through shortly. I have been checking my email like a hawk the past few weeks waiting for anything to update on the back end. If I had to guess, the interview would be set for Sept.

---

Sept. 4th, I received an invite for the in person assessment. All of the dates were for Sept leaving very little time to decide which date would work best. With all of the dates being in Sept, I did not have the ability to book a flex day or any time of time off.

I chose to go with Sept. 25th, 2026 for the in person assessment centre interview. I managed to connect with a few of the July 13th FEAST takers and I know that one did the in person assessment for Sept. 9th. I reached out to check on how it went, it's hard not to look at follow up questions on the process but I want to come into this one as blind as possible.

I have been using AI bots to work on generalized interview questions and for help on creating solid answers to any of the general STAR questions that are normally asked during the interviews.

I am still very much in the dark on what the group and personal activities will look like though.

Similar to my FEAST attempt in 2022, this one is done right at the Toronto base. I hope that I will get the chance to do some general plan spotting before and after the test as a way to decompress before the drive home.

---

Sept. 25th I showed up early. I left my house at 5:50 AM and ended up making it to Toronto around 7. The assessment centre started at 8 AM.

I got to the Toronto base around 7:30 and I met with the other 2 people completing the assessment centre with me.

During the assessment, we started with a brief introduction followed by a series of group games / 2-people games. After this, we had a very detailed breakdown of the training process. 

After, we started the official interview. I was up first. Overall, it's a general series of STAR like questions. Some are more free form while others are more rapid fire. You get ~25 minutes for the interview.

Honestly looking back, I think I did okay. I could have definitely done better on some of the group assessments, but I was able to keep a clear line of communication and bounce back from any mistakes that I made during the process.

It appeared that the other people who attended also completed their FEAST test in July like myself. If the groups were only 3 big it would mean that only 15 people got the invite for this current period.

The wait begins. Another test taker I was chatting with on Reddit did the test earlier in the month and has already received the rejection email. Time will tell where I land though.

----

Oct 5th, 2026, still waiting for the results of the interview. I have been spam refreshing the workday along side my email every day since the interview.

I was talking to another person on Reddit who completed their in person assessment 1 day before me. They received their results on Thursday afternoon, Oct. 1th, 2026.

Hopefully the results will come through shortly because I am starting to get a little stressed out over this. I would like to know the answer either way at this point to free up some space in my mind.

---

Oct 6th, the status moved back to Assessment. This should mean the results will be in any hour now.

I've seen another post on Reddit where it moved to assessment and the person found out very shortly after.

Another person on Reddit I have been chatting with had her's tick over yesterday and just got an email today at ~1PM that she did not make it.

Has me a little worried, but even so, we push forward.

---

Oct 8th, received the rejection email that I was not successful.

Overall, the process was fun and I will give it another try in 3 years. It's hard to say where I went wrong but with the process being what it is, it's difficult to figure out where I could have improved.

Although I may have not been successful this time around, I hope that anyone reading through this in the future can achieve their goal or learn from my experience.
